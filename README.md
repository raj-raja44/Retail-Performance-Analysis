# 📊 Retail Business Performance & Profitability Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes retail sales data to identify sales trends, profitability patterns, top-performing products, and regional performance.

The analysis was created to help businesses understand their sales performance and make better data-driven decisions.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Power BI
- Microsoft Excel
- Data Cleaning
- Data Visualization
- Business Analysis

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance
- Identify the most profitable product categories
- Analyze sales trends over time
- Compare regional performance
- Identify underperforming products and categories
- Understand the relationship between sales and profit

## 📈 Key Features

- Interactive Power BI dashboard
- Sales and profit analysis
- Category and sub-category analysis
- Regional performance analysis
- Time-based sales trends
- Product-level analysis
- Interactive filters and slicers

## 🔍 Key Insights

- Technology and Office Supplies were among the top-performing categories.
- Sales showed seasonal variations throughout the year.
- Some regions and sub-categories generated lower profitability.
- High sales volume does not always result in high profit.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Focus on highly profitable product categories.
- Investigate low-profit products and regions.
- Use seasonal sales patterns for better inventory planning.
- Monitor products with high sales but low profitability.
